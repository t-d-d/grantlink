# Independent and unbranded
Relevance: The page, site/**, the README, images, colours or fonts, or any text that names Cancer Research UK
Decision: Cancer Research UK is named only to say whose grants grantlink reads. Every view carries the independence notice, and nothing uses CRUK branding.

Why: grantlink is an independent demonstration on public data. A reader
must never take it for a Cancer Research UK service, or think the charity
endorses it.

## Rules

- Every view of the page carries, in its footer, the notice that grantlink
  is an independent demonstration on public data from Europe PMC, not
  affiliated with or endorsed by Cancer Research UK, and the address for
  corrections.
- The README carries the same notice.
- No file in the repo is a CRUK logo, and the page uses no CRUK colours,
  fonts or styling.
- The live tier checks the deployed page for the notice after each deploy.

## Do not

- Put the notice on one page only. A reader may arrive at any view.
- Borrow CRUK's look to make the page feel familiar to a CRUK panel. It
  reads as endorsement.

## Judgment

No check can tell a CRUK colour from any other colour. The page's palette
comes from the mock-ups (#16), and a change to it is for review.

# Accurate, and correctable
Relevance: The page's words about the links, each link's status, the warranty, or the address for corrections
Decision: The page says its links are proposed by software, labels each one with its status, gives no warranty, and gives an address for corrections.

Why: The links are guesses made by rules, and later a model, from text
written for people. A reader must not take one for a fact a funder has
confirmed, and anyone named in a wrong link must have a way to have it
fixed.

## Rules

- The page says, before any run and beside the list, that the links are
  proposed by software and come with no warranty.
- Every listed link carries its status. Until a person reviews it, the
  status is "Proposed by software".
- Every view of the page gives thomas.david.dickinson@gmail.com for
  corrections and removals (independence.md).
- Each link will show its confidence once the model proposes links.

## Do not

- Call a link confirmed or accepted before a person has reviewed it.
- Drop the status column on a phone to save space. The table scrolls in
  its own box instead.

## Judgment

The checks read the words on the page. Whether a new piece of text claims
more certainty than the service has is for review.
